\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Multi-\/vehicle world: owns every \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_race_car}{Race\+Car} and steps them together. 

The \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator}{Simulator} handles what a single \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_race_car}{Race\+Car} cannot do alone: stepping all cars together, detecting car-\/to-\/car collisions, and making cars visible in each other\textquotesingle{}s Li\+DAR scans. \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1f110__env_1_1_f110_env}{F110\+Env} wraps this class and adds lap counting, rendering and the gym interface on top.

Detect car-\/to-\/car collisions with the GJK algorithm. 

Each car is modelled as a length x width rectangle at its current pose. Every pair of rectangles is tested for overlap with GJK (Gilbert-\/\+Johnson-\/\+Keerthi), a fast test for intersecting convex shapes.

Overwrites \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_a2266104910535f06f31c1212e62fde8b}{collisions} and \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_a36360908455ca6fb36242684c2ad5b73}{collision\+\_\+idx}. Wall collisions are added to \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_a2266104910535f06f31c1212e62fde8b}{collisions} afterwards in \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_abbfac2b093e12f8a42a15730df277470}{step()}. \Hypertarget{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_afe92713e94ca33e090e124e21c39318b}\index{f110\_gym.envs.base\_classes.Simulator@{f110\_gym.envs.base\_classes.Simulator}!reset@{reset}}

Advance the whole world by one physics time step. 

The steps are:
\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Move every car (\doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_race_car_a7a2d1fe796b39619a539b69411265c5c}{Race\+Car.\+update\+\_\+pose}) and record its new pose. Each car also takes a scan against the static map.
\item Check car-\/to-\/car collisions on the new poses (check\+\_\+collision).
\item For each car: give it the other cars\textquotesingle{} poses, check for a wall collision, and add the other cars to its scan (\doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_race_car_ad6c4539dfae27037296311db875230b0}{Race\+Car.\+update\+\_\+scan}). A wall collision also sets that car\textquotesingle{}s entry in \doxylink{classf110__gym_1_1envs_1_1base__classes_1_1_simulator_a2266104910535f06f31c1212e62fde8b}{collisions}.
\item Build and return the observation dictionary.
\end{DoxyEnumerate}
