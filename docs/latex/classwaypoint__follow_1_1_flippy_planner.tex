\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Test planner that flips its steering left and right to stress the integrator and dynamics. 

For testing only. It was used to expose numerical errors when the dynamic (slip) model is active at low speed.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
{\ttfamily counter} is never incremented, so the steering flips on {\itshape every} call regardless of {\ttfamily flip\+\_\+every}. The default steer of 2 rad is far beyond the car\textquotesingle{}s steering limit, so the steering simply swings between its limits. 
\end{DoxyNote}
