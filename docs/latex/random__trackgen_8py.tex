\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Generate random closed race tracks as gym maps (image + YAML) plus a centreline CSV. 

Adapted from the track generator in Open\+AI gym\textquotesingle{}s Car\+Racing-\/v0.\hypertarget{random__trackgen_8py_random_trackgen_usage}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Usage}{Usage}}\label{random__trackgen_8py_random_trackgen_usage}

\begin{DoxyCode}{0}
\DoxyCodeLine{python\ random\_trackgen.py\ -\/-\/seed\ 123\ -\/-\/num\_maps\ 5}

\end{DoxyCode}
 Output goes into folders created in the current directory:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\ttfamily maps/\+map\+N.\+png} and {\ttfamily maps/\+map\+N.\+pgm}: the track walls as a black-\/on-\/white image.
\item {\ttfamily maps/\+map\+N.\+yaml}: map metadata (resolution 0.\+0625 m/\+pixel, origin).
\item {\ttfamily centerline/\+map\+N.\+csv}: centreline waypoints "{}x, y"{} in metres, starting at (0, 0).
\end{DoxyItemize}\hypertarget{random__trackgen_8py_random_trackgen_algorithm}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{How a track is made (create\+\_\+track())}{How a track is made (create\+\_\+track())}}\label{random__trackgen_8py_random_trackgen_algorithm}

\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item Place 16 checkpoints around a circle, each at a random angle within its 1/\+16 sector and a random radius.
\item "{}\+Drive"{} a point around them: at each step it turns toward the next checkpoint, with a limited turn rate, then moves forward a fixed distance. Every step is recorded.
\item Cut out exactly one lap: the section between two consecutive passes through the start angle. If its two ends don\textquotesingle{}t meet up cleanly, give up (the caller retries).
\item Offset the centreline by +/\+-\/\+WIDTH with shapely to get the two walls.
\end{DoxyEnumerate}

convert\+\_\+track() then plots the walls with matplotlib and writes the image, YAML and centreline files.

convert\+\_\+track (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{}]{track}{, }\item[{}]{track\+\_\+int}{, }\item[{}]{track\+\_\+ext}{, }\item[{}]{iter}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Render a generated track to a gym map and save its files (see the file overview for the outputs). 

The two walls are drawn as thick black lines on a white 20 x 20 inch matplotlib figure, saved at 80 dpi (1600 x 1600 pixels), and converted to grayscale with Open\+CV.

The world frame is chosen so the first centreline point is at (0, 0). Pixel positions are measured with matplotlib\textquotesingle{}s trans\+Data, which works at the figure\textquotesingle{}s own 100 dpi, and scaled by 0.\+05 m per pixel. At the saved 80 dpi that is 0.\+05 \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*} 100 / 80 = 0.\+0625 m per pixel, which is the resolution written to the YAML.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The 0.\+05 / 0.\+0625 relationship relies on matplotlib\textquotesingle{}s default figure dpi of 100. A matplotlibrc that changes it would misalign the centreline and the image. 

The "{}map size"{} printed is the figure size at 100 dpi, not the saved image size. 
\end{DoxyNote}
