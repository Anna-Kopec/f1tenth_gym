Choose the point to aim for. 


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item If the car is within {\ttfamily lookahead\+\_\+distance} of the raceline: the raceline point one lookahead distance ahead (see the note).
\item If it is further away but within {\ttfamily max\+\_\+reacquire} (20 m): the nearest raceline segment\textquotesingle{}s start point, to steer back onto the line.
\item Otherwise: None.
\end{DoxyItemize}


\begin{DoxyParams}{Parameters}
{\em waypoints} & Full raceline array (all columns), used for the speed column. \\
\hline
{\em lookahead\+\_\+distance} & Lookahead distance (m). \\
\hline
{\em position} & np.\+ndarray of shape (2,): car position. \\
\hline
{\em theta} & Car yaw (unused). \\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
\begin{DoxyReturn}{Returns}
np.\+ndarray of shape (3,): \+[x, y, target speed], or None.
\end{DoxyReturn}
\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The point returned is the {\itshape start} waypoint of the segment where the lookahead circle crosses, not the exact crossing point. The speed is taken from the waypoint nearest the car. 
\end{DoxyNote}
