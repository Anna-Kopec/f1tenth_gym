Update lap counts and times, and decide whether the episode is over. 

{\bfseries{Finish zone:}} each car\textquotesingle{}s position is expressed relative to its own start position, in a frame aligned with the {\itshape ego} car\textquotesingle{}s starting heading:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item forward (along the start heading): dx
\item sideways: dy, but anything within +/\+-\/2 m counts as 0, and beyond that only the excess over 2 m counts.
\end{DoxyItemize}

A car is "{}in the zone"{} when dx\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 + (excess dy)\texorpdfstring{$^\wedge$}{\string^}2 \texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}= 0.\+1. That is roughly a box about 0.\+63 m long (+/\+-\/0.32 m) and 4 m wide, centred on the start pose.

{\bfseries{Lap counting:}} every time a car enters or leaves the zone, its toggle count goes up by 1. Cars start inside the zone, so leaving and coming back is 2 toggles, i.\+e. one lap. A car\textquotesingle{}s lap time keeps updating until it reaches 4 toggles (2 laps), then freezes.

{\bfseries{Done}} when the ego car has collided, or every car has completed 2 laps.

\item {\ttfamily checkpoint\+\_\+done} (np.\+ndarray of bool, shape (num\+\_\+agents,)): whether each car has finished 2 laps.
