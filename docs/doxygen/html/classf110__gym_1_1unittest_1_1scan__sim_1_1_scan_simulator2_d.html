Parameters
num_beamsNumber of beams per scan.
fovField of view (rad).
std_devStandard deviation of the Gaussian noise added to each beam (m).
epsSphere-tracing stop tolerance (m).
theta_disNumber of discrete angles in the 0 to 2*pi lookup table.
max_rangeMaximum range (m). Used to stop tracing, but results are not clipped to it.
seedSeed for the noise generator.
